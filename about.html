Where the data comes from

IdentityMake WP Slack accounts are created from WordPress.org accounts, so the match between the two is exact, not guessed.
ActivityPer-person message search across every public channel, thread replies included.
Supporter workThe application bot logs who processed each WordCamp and Meetup application. Weekly mentor check-in replies count too.
LocationEach person's own WordPress.org profile, geocoded. No profile location, no dot — nothing is guessed.

What it misses

Help Scout tickets, private channels, and anything off Slack and WordPress.org. Someone can be quiet here and busy elsewhere — the map tells you who to ask, not what's going on in their life. When you know better than the data, click the person, hit Update contributor, and say what you know. Corrections beat every automated source and carry your name and reason.
